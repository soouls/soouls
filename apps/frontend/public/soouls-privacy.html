What we collect

Short version: your entries, your account details, and basic usage data. Nothing more than we need to run the product.

When you create an account, we collect your email address, a display name if you choose to set one, and authentication data. When you use Soouls, we store the entries you write, record, or draw, along with metadata like timestamps and which capture method you used.

  • Account information: email, display name, password hash (we never store your plaintext password)
  • Content you create: text entries, voice recordings, images, doodles, and tasks
  • Usage data: feature interactions, crash logs, and performance metrics, collected in aggregate
  • Device information: operating system and app version, used only for compatibility and support

How we use it

We use your account information to operate your account and communicate with you about your subscription, security, and product updates you've opted into. We use your content strictly to power the features you've asked for: displaying your Spatial Canvas, generating your Sunday Review, and surfacing Thought Clusters.

We use aggregate, de-identified usage data to understand which features are helpful and which aren't — for example, knowing that voice capture is used heavily late at night helps us prioritize improvements there, without us ever reading what was said in any specific recording.

Encryption & storage

Entries are encrypted at rest and in transit. We built the system so that even we can't casually browse your journal.

All entries are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest, and TLS 1.3 in transit between your device and our servers. Access to production data is restricted to a small number of engineers under audit-logged, time-boxed access for the specific purpose of debugging a reported issue — never for browsing.

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data or your entries, under any circumstance, to anyone. We do not share your content with advertisers, and Soouls has no advertising product to sell it to. We use a small number of infrastructure vendors (cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing) who process data strictly on our behalf under data processing agreements, and who are contractually barred from using it for their own purposes.

  • Cloud hosting: stores encrypted data; cannot read entry content
  • Payment processor: receives billing information only, never journal content
  • Email delivery: receives your email address only, for transactional messages

AI & insight processing

Your entries are never used to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's.

Sunday Review, Thought Clusters, and Emotional Insights are generated by processing your entries through language and pattern-recognition models to produce output shown only to you. This processing happens within our infrastructure under the same access restrictions as the rest of your data, and the output is never retained by any underlying model provider beyond the single request needed to generate your insight.

Your rights & controls

From Settings → Data & Ownership, you can export a full copy of your entries in an open format at any time, and request permanent deletion of your account and all associated content. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days, including removal from backups.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to object to certain processing and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us using the details below to exercise any of these rights.

Retention & deletion

We retain your content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your content is removed from active systems within 30 days and from backup systems within 90 days. If you downgrade a paid plan rather than delete your account, your existing entries remain fully intact and exportable.

Children's privacy

Soouls is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify you by email and display a notice in the app before the changes take effect. Continued use of Soouls after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@soouls.in. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
